Common queries regarding US mortgages, interest rates, and debt payoff.

Refinancing federal or private student loans can secure a lower interest rate and reduce monthly burdens. However, refinancing federal loans into private ones means forfeiting federal benefits like income-driven repayment and forgiveness programs.
